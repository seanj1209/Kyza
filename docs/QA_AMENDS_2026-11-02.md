# QA review: AMENDS - KYZA

Scope: six amendments from the supplied PDF plus the owner's K Seal screenshot amendment. Review branch `feat/pdf-qa-amends` only. Owner acceptance is pending; no merge or Beta release is authorised.

## Included

1. Plain Film renders supported editable elements while excluding the entire Openings and Die Cut Outs groups. Seals are optional, and eyemarks may move anywhere inside the cutter. Export validation retains checks for invalid dimensions, negative seals and out-of-bounds marks.
2. Removed Gusset from Basic elements. Construction-specific gusset controls remain in their pack docks.
3. Strip Film/Laminate has a per-artboard Pre-Slit View in More cutter tools. It shows cutter outlines, measurements and offcuts without technical elements. SVG, Illustrator-compatible SVG and PDF use the selected view; the underlying project is preserved. Zero seals and no eyemarks are permitted.
4. New strip cutters default to 15 mm seals with dashed, unfilled outlines at 70% opacity. One top eyemark is 10 x 5 mm, inset 1.5 mm. Brown/white stock uses black; black stock uses white. Stock changes update automatic contrast while retaining custom ink colours.
5. Fold Lines exposes horizontal/vertical direction and editable offsets. Horizontal lines span the cutter edge to edge, share the vertical style and support dragging. Offset means millimetres from cutter top and is checked against cutter height. Existing saved horizontal offsets now take effect (the previous renderer always centred them). Width submeasures use vertical folds only.
6. Help Me Choose and the Flow dock use the supplied flagship Flow artwork, with provenance recorded in the branding manifest.
7. K Seal excludes the inherited DOY panel-width measurement row that caused the marked 190/100 mm overlap. Native K Seal spans and geometry remain unchanged; legitimate 100/190 mm spans on differently sized cutters are retained.
