# GLOSSARY_PACKAGING

## Terms

- `seal zone`: Area reserved for sealing. Important artwork or functional features must not intrude into it.
- `safe zone`: Area inside the usable panel where important content may be placed with reduced production risk.
- `live area`: Area intended for meaningful printed or functional content after trim and clearance rules are applied.
- `live width`: Usable width inside the relevant boundaries after subtracting trim, seal, and other restricted areas.
- `bleed`: Extra artwork area that extends beyond trim so edge finishing does not leave gaps.
- `trim`: Final cut boundary of the finished piece.
- `gusset`: Folded expansion panel used to add depth to a pouch or pack.
- `gusset line`: The line that defines where the gusset folds or transitions.
- `fold line`: Any intended bend or fold reference line in the structure.
- `zipper flange`: Material area used to attach a zipper feature.
- `zipper flange clear`: Minimum clearance that must remain around the zipper flange for placement safety.
- `tear notch`: Small cut feature that helps the pack open.
- `tear notch exclusion`: Area around the tear notch that must remain clear of conflicting features.
- `eye mark`: Registration mark used by print or converting equipment for alignment.
- `half-moon cutout`: Semicircular cut feature, usually used for access or opening assistance.
- `hanger slot`: Slot or hole pattern that allows the pack to hang on retail fixtures.
- `window`: Defined area intended to remain clear or differently treated to show product.
- `perf`: Perforation feature made from repeated cuts or weak points to guide tearing.
